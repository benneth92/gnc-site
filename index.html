// TECHNICAL RESPONSIBILITIES

DAILY TASK BREAKDOWN

⌕
🗣️
Team Meetings & Briefings

Daily standups, design reviews, and program briefings keep the team synchronized on schedule, open issues, and priority shifts.

  • Agile standups and sprint planning
  • Engineering review boards (ERBs)
  • Program milestone briefings
  • Interface alignment meetings with subsystems
📐
Reviewing Design Specifications

Daily review of the latest wire harness drawings, ICDs, and connector specifications to catch conflicts before they reach fabrication.

  • Wire harness routing drawings and BOM review
  • ICD and interface data sheet analysis
  • MIL-SPEC / AS9100 compliance checks
  • ECO review and disposition
💻
CAD Design Work

Hands-on harness routing in 3D CAD tools — placing connectors, routing wire bundles, managing bend radii and clamp spacing to meet form-fit-function requirements.

  • 3D harness routing and clamp placement
  • Connector pinout assignment and verification
  • Clearance and bend radius checks
  • Drawing releases and revision control
🤝
Cross-Functional Collaboration

Constant interface work with avionics, structures, mechanical, and systems teams to resolve conflicts, align on requirements, and validate integration approaches.

  • Avionics integration coordination
  • Structural interface and clamp negotiation
  • Systems-level requirements traceability
  • Ground support equipment coordination
🔧
Prototyping & Testing

Lab-based work building, inspecting, and testing prototype harnesses — from first-article inspection to continuity and insulation resistance testing.

  • Harness assembly and first-article inspection
  • Continuity and dielectric withstand testing
  • Connector insertion/extraction force checks
  • Functional power-up verification
📊
Simulation & Analysis

Analytical work covering power loads, EMI/EMC risk, signal integrity, and thermal margin to validate harness designs before hardware build.

  • Power distribution and load analysis
  • EMI/EMC susceptibility assessments
  • Signal integrity and crosstalk analysis
  • Thermal derating and wire sizing
🛠️
Troubleshooting & Design Adjustments

Investigating test failures, integration anomalies, and field reports to identify root cause and disposition corrective action through the design or process.

  • Fault isolation and root cause analysis
  • Design change disposition (ECO/DRB)
  • Chafe protection and routing rework
  • Non-conformance report (NCR) processing
📦
Supplier Coordination & Documentation

Manages the supply chain interface: issuing RFQs, reviewing supplier data packages, validating material certifications, and keeping documentation current.

  • RFQ issuance and supplier selection support
  • Material and specification conformance review
  • Supplier data package (SDP) audits
  • CDRL and technical data package updates

Senior Wire Harness Engineer

The most direct progression — own complex harness designs end-to-end, mentor junior engineers, drive standards and best practices. Deepens technical authority in electrical design and integration.

Systems Engineer
SENIOR ▼

Leverage integration and ICD experience to move into system-level requirements, architecture, and V&V. Your harness background makes you uniquely credible at interface boundaries.

MBSE Requirements V&V Plans Architecture
📋
Project Engineer / Project Manager
LEAD ▼

Transition technical depth into program leadership — managing schedule, cost, and risk across harness and avionics deliverables. Strong fit for engineers who enjoy stakeholder coordination.

EVM Risk Management IMP/IMS CDR/PDR
🔌
Lead Electrical Engineer
LEAD ▼

Own the electrical architecture across a vehicle or system — power distribution, grounding, bonding, and EMC strategy. Leadership role that expands well beyond harness into full EE scope.

Power Architecture Grounding EMC Strategy Team Lead
🎯
Technical Specialist / SME
SENIOR ▼

Become the organization's go-to expert in wire harness design, connector selection, or avionics wiring standards. SME roles carry high influence with minimal management overhead.

MIL-SPEC Authority Design Reviews Standards Bodies

Aerospace R&D Engineer
SENIOR ▼

Apply hands-on experience to next-generation programs — advanced materials, optical fiber harnesses, high-voltage aircraft, or novel connector technologies for DARPA/AFRL programs.

Advanced Concepts IRAD/CRAD Technology Maturation
