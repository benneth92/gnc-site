Professional Profile

Benneth Perez

Benneth Perez is a Guidance, Navigation, and Controls engineer working on advanced aerospace and defense systems that require high-performance estimation, control, and mission-critical autonomy.

His technical work spans guidance laws, navigation algorithms, control architectures, state estimation, tracking concepts, and system-level integration for complex flight systems operating in demanding environments.

Previously, Benneth served as an Avionics Hardware Systems Engineer at The Boeing Company, where he built a strong foundation in wire harness design, avionics integration, installation engineering, and test for advanced aerospace and defense platforms.

His background connects hands-on aerospace hardware integration with modern GN&C, estimation, software-informed analysis, and autonomous systems engineering.
